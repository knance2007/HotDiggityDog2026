## My Hot Dog has a First Name....

Hot dog nicknames have evolved many times over the years. Some are funny, creative, vulgar, and some only make sense to the person who came up with them. Some nicknames are regional, some are as old as the hot dog its self, and A LOT come from social media and the meme community, 
	
	The nickname "Glizzy" seemed to explode onto the scene in recent years. People have built onto this nickname with other names such as "glizzy gladiator", "glizzy goblin" and so on. As you can tell, these names are typically reserved for someone that loves a hot dog.
