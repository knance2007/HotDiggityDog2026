
## Hot Dog Humor and More

Believe it or not, hot dogs have been a part of pop culture for many more years that people may think about. From message rooms like AOL and MSN chat to fashion and art, to exploding onto the social media scene with TikTok trends in recent years. 

### Hot Dog Eras 
- The Early Years -Hot Dogs or Legs?
- Snapchat Enters- The Dancing Hot Dog Filter
- Hot Dogs turn into Glizzies
- High Fashion Takes Over
- TikTok and Vine Trends 

The Hot Dog Internet Sensation we all are aware of now didn't grow on its own. The nickname "Glizzy" and others became part of internet slang that is used frequently, and can be read more in depth about in [[Hot Dogs and Nicknames]]. TikTok, Vine, and Snapchat also helped the hot dog become the popular meme we know it to be. [[Hot Dogs Being an Internet Sensation]] has much more information about this and funny facts about how people have embraced the hot dog. Movies, Tv Shows, and Fashion Designers have all featured the hot dog in some shape or form. Fashion has helped portray the hot dog in a new and fun way.
