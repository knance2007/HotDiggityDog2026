## Hot Dog Festivities- Competitions and Challenges

Have you thought "How  many hot dogs could a person eat in one sitting?" Or " How many hot dogs could I even eat?" Below is a list of challenges and eating competitions you could enter to find out! 

### Nathan's Famous: The Big One 

Coney Island becomes a spectacle every 4th of July to watch this food competition. Competitors from all over to see who is the top hot dog consumer. Watching this is exciting, terrifying, and wildly dramatic. 

Multiple techniques are used by the competitors for the 10 minute long competition. 
- The most famous is the "Dunk and Bite Method". This technique involves dunking your hot dog into water or any other non-alcholic drink to lessen chewing time and ease swallowing.
