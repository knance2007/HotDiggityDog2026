## Lets Take a Bite Out of Hot Dog Trivia!

#### When is National Hot Dog Day in the U.S.A?
The Third Wednesday in July! Though it does vary depending on National Hot Dog Month and the North American Meat Institute. It is usually the third Wednesday in July.

#### What year did Nathan's Famous first open?
1916!! The original Nathan's restaurant is on the corner of Surf and Stillwell Avenues in Coney Island. 

#### Who invented the hot dog bun?
Charles Feltman, a german immigrant began selling sausages in rolls at his stand in Coney Island. More on this topic is over on [[Whats in a name]].
