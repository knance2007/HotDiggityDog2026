## Family and Friend Recommendations 

Hot Dogs are a community food. When I say "community food", I mean that friends and family gather at BBQs, birthdays, tailgate parties to eat a great hot dog and other yummy sides. (more info on sides can be found at [[Sides and Such]])

I have asked multiple friends, family, and co workers where they can get their favorite hot dogs from. 

My co-worker and friend Magda votes her favorite hot dog currently is the Bulgogi Dog from Haute Dog TX and Pinky's Bar in Temple, Texas. This hot dog consists of beef hot dog, bulgogi beef, spicy mayo, cabbage, and kimchi. 
![[The bulgogi Dog Pinkys.png]]

Our IT director Fred voted that he likes to make his hot dogs at home with Omaha Steaks All Beef Franks. He is quoted as saying "You only need one to fill ya up!"

One attorney Jessi says her favorite dog is the Ditty Dog, fully loaded. She gets this hot dog at Ditty Dog in Austin, Texas. This hot dog comes with cream cheese, honey-mayo, crunchy toppings, caramelized onions, Asian-ketchup, chipotle mustard, sriracha, and green sauce on top of your choice of a beef dog or vegan dog. 
![[Screenshot_29-9-2026_17552_www.dittydog.com.jpeg]]


My friend Shane votes that his favorite hot dog is a chili cheese dog with mustard from a place called "The Better Burger" in Odessa, Texas. To quote him, "they have fire glizzys". He also enjoys Sonic and Sam's Club hot dogs. 

My brother Christopher says his favorite hot dog is from stand outside the Corona Club in Mexico. "I'd pay $20 for one of their wieners" he says. 

The managing attorney for my office says one of his favorite hot dogs is the MX Street Dog from The Dogfather in San Antonio, TX. This is a bacon wrapped hot dog, topped with grilled onions and peppers, crushed flaming hot Cheetos, mustard, mayo and a fire roasted jalapeno. They do have a vegan option by choosing the vegan frank and subbing the hot Cheetos for Takis and the mayo for chipotle mayo.
