### Top 3 States to Grab a Dog
- New York 
	- New York is KNOWN for its classic dogs. you have: 
	- Nathan's Famous located on Coney Island. Its been around since 1916 and also hosts the annual Nathan's Hot Dog Eating Contest see[[Hot Dogs and Pop Culture]]
	- Gray's Papaya established in 1973. Known for its hot dogs and papaya drinks and its "Recession Special" which is two hot dogs and papaya juice at a low price. 
- Arizona
	- Arizona has its own special dog- wrapped in bacon and multiple toppings including mustard, onions, and jalepeno peppers. 
	- El Guero Canelo is Arizona's top spot for this special dog. 
- Illinois 
	- Known for the Chicago Style dog. Their signature hot dog is an all beef dog on a poppy seed bun with relish,, mustard, celery salt, and onions, tomato, and peppers.
