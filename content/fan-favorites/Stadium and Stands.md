## Hot Dogs! Get Ya Hot Dogs!

If there's one place I can grab a hot dog, it's the baseball field. Simple to Sophisticated, here's a list of the Top 5 Baseball Parks to get a nice hot dog. 

#### Dodger Stadium and the Dodger Dog 
This dog remains one of the most famous baseball hot dogs. An all pork wiener, brought in fresh everyday. Then boiled and grilled to order. Toppings are yours to choose from. They even have a footlong option if you're REALLY hungry. 

#### Fenway Park and the Fenway Franks
This ballpark is home to the Boston Red Sox, built in 1912. These hot dogs are famous for their char-grilled flavor and come with multiple toppings of your choosing. 


#### Wrigley Field and the Chicago Dog
This field is home to the Chicago Cubs. This field sells the "chicago style hot dog" This dog comes dressed with mustard, onions, relish, a pickle spear, and peppers on a poppy seed bun. 


#### Oriole Park at Camden Yard and the Crab Mac Dog 
This ball park is home to the Baltimore Orioles and one hefty hot dog. If you're looking to go all out, this is the dog for you. This hot dog is served at Stuggy's and its a perfect hot dog topped with creamy, melty crab mac and cheese. Crab mac and cheese? YES PLEASE. On top of a hot dog? Even. Better. 

#### Angel Stadium and the Angel's Cali Dog 
The Los Angeles Angel's have the perfect hot dog. I honestly think this should be number one. This hot dog is wrapped in BACON. Then covered in carne asada, pico de gallo, and jalepenos with a little bit of sour cream. Oh. and fries on top. Can it get any better than meat wrapped in more meat, covered in MORE meat? I dont think so.
