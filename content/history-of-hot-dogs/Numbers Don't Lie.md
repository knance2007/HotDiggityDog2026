## How Many Hot Dogs are we eating?

According to the National Hot Dog and Sausage Council, Americans consume an astounding number of hot dogs. Lets take a look at the numbers.

### By The Pound 

According to the data collected for the year 2025, 905 million pounds of hot dogs were purchased at stores. 

In Los Angeles, they consume more hot dogs than any other city coming in at a strong 27.8 MILLION pounds. 




### Dolla Dolla Bills Yall 
Now lets look at the amount of dollars spent on the delicious dog. 

During the summer, between holidays such as Memorial Day and Labor Day an estimated amount of $1.6 BILLION is sold. 
10% of hot dog sales happen in July, which is designated as National Hot Dog Month. 

As stated above, in 2025 905 million pounds of hot dogs were sold in stores. That comes out to more than $3 BILLION in retail sales.
