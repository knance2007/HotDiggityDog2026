## Whats in a name?
In the US, hot dog refers to both the meat and the combination of the meat and a bun. 

In New Zealand, a hot dog is a wiener dipped in batter and fried. What the US would normally call a corn dog.
 ![[New Zealand.jpg]]

In France, a hot dog can be made with a baguette that has been hollowed out in the middle, with the wiener inside. (toppings if you want of course) 
![[French Hot_Dog.jpg|159]]


In Thailand, they prepare a crepe with a sausage inside. This is called khanom tokiao.
