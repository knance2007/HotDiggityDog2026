## Sandwich? Yes or No?

A debate that has gone on for ages is whether or not to classify a hot dog as a sandwich, or something else. 

The National Hot Dog and Sausage Society have some thoughts on the matter. 

On their website, they state "Limiting the hot dogs significance by saying it's just a sandwich is like calling the Dalai Lama just a guy". 

Below are a few reasons why the NHDSC believes that the hot dog is more than "just a sandwich" :

1. Marlene Deitrich didn't say that champagne and sandwiches were her favorite, she said champagne and hot dogs. 
2. There are never any myths or mystique about what's in a sandwich.
3. If you won the lottery, you wouldn't say "Sandwich!" You would say "Hot Dog!!"
4. There was never a campaign for a sandwich emoji
5. DOES ESPN BROADCAST SANDWICH EATING CONTESTS???


In the end, I think we can all agree that a sandwich isn't a hot dog, and a hot dog isn't a sandwich.
