# History of the Hot Dog!
Hot off the roller, steamer, the grill. The history of the hot dog and how it became a beloved food to the young and old. 

## Where do we begin?
The Hot Dogs history in the U.S. of A. begins allegedly in the late 1800s, early 1900s with a concession stand and dream. Legend has it, that a German immigrant from Frankfurt started using a bun to hold the sausages after his wife suggested it so that customers could hold the sausage without dirtying their hands.  Another account states that Charles Feltman started serving them in 1871 at Coney Island, New York.
