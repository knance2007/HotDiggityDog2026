## History and The Hot Dog 

Hot Dogs have come a long way from the 1800s, but exactly how far? Lets take a look. 


Hot Dogs were first sold at baseball games in 1893. 

Mickey Mouse's first words on screen were "Hot Dog!" (I know you read that in his voice)

Hot dogs were one of the first foods eaten on the moon! "To Infinity and Beyond!" Buzz Lightyear would probably love a hot dog while traveling through space. 

Soviet Premier Nikita Khrushchev during a visit to the U.S. in 1959 admitted that Americans made better sausages.
