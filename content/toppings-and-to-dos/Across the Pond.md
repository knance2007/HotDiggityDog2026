## Interesting Toppings Around the World 

Ever wonder what hot dogs are like in other parts of the world? Wonder no more! 

In Sweden hot dogs are served topped with mashed potatoes and shrimp salad. Typically wrapped in flat bread and sprinkled with fried onions. These are called tunnbrödrulle. 

![[tunnsbrödrulle-1-1024x1536.jpg]]


In Iceland, there is a hot dog stand called Baejarin's Betzu Hot Dog Stand. They have a "must try" called a "psylur med ollu" which means hot dog with everything. Toppings include raw onions, crispy fried onions, ketchup, sweet mustard, and remoulade. 
Fun Fact: Their ketchup is made out of apples. This gives the ketchup a distintive sweetness while having less sugar than American ketchup.
