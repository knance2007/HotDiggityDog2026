## Hot Dog Mixings 
When you think of other foods that contain hot dogs, your thoughts probably jump to a gelatin mold with hot dog slices you saw in a horror movie, or your Aunt Susan's kitchen.  But there are so many (better) recipes that use hot dogs! 

Did you have a bbq in your backyard and now you have left over grilled dogs? There's plenty  of things that you can use them in. 

Starting off strong, we have Beenie Weenies. 
A staple recipe in my house growing up. 
Easy mixture of pork n' beans and cut up hot dog weenies. Put in a pot and cook until steamy and hot. Or you can eat it cold, but its much better hot. 

Another recipe that I am not opposed to on a weekend, is hot dog tater tot casserole. 
This casserole has all the good bits from a chili cheese dog in casserole form. Mix chili, cheese, sliced hot dogs and tater tots, cover with more cheese and bake until golden brown and bubbly. 

One recipe I remember growing up and still make to this day, is one my mom made A LOT. I call them baked hot dog sandwiches. They may have an ACTUAL name, I'll never know. This recipe is a piece of bread, slice of cheese, mustard and a hot dog. Sit the dog on the cheese and bread diagonal, pull the corners of the bread up and slide in a toothpick to hold the bread together. Pop in the oven until the cheese is just melted and the bread is toasty. Dip in mustard and enjoy! 

## OR 
you could do as my niece did when she was younger, and just eat them cold right out of the fridge. I think my sister went through a package a week! Not my preferred method, but girl loves her hot dogs.
