## Mustard vs. Ketchup- who wins?

See also: [[Mashed — Hot Dog Condiment Poll]]

Tale as old as time. Is Mustard or Ketchup better on a hot dog? This is a topic highly debated all over the world. 

In the good ol U.S. of A, mustard seems to be the top contender between the two options. (don't get me started on mayo. no thanks.)
Even the National Hot Dog and Sausage Council (yes it's a real thing) has strict guidelines on what to put on your hot dog. 
One rule states " don't use ketchup on your hot dog after the age of 18. Mustard, relish, onions, cheese and chili are acceptable" Dramatic? Maybe. Who am I to argue with an entire Hot Dog Council? 

### What About Other Countries?
In countries such as Venezuela and Denmark, you will find many people using ketchup as topping. In other countries like Germany and Guatemala, mustard remains the top condiment. 

Iceland loves mustard on their hot dogs so much that they created a special mustard for them. This mustard is called Pylsusinnep, made from brown mustard seeds that has a sweet but tangy flavor. 

### Polls and Voting 
The NHDSC held a poll to see how consumers enjoy their hot dogs. 
- Nearly 71% of Americans prefer mustard
- 52% prefer ketchup
- 47% prefer onions 
- 45% enjoy chili 
- and 41% enjoy relish. 

Another poll was taken to see how Americans feel about ketchup as a topping. 
The results are as follows:
- 79% say its an acceptable topping 
- 21% say absolutely not.
