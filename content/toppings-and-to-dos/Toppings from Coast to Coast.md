# Who wants a hot dog??!!

Condiments and toppings vary depending on which state or side of the US you're on. 


## Toppings from Coast to Coast

In the South, chili is the most frequently used topping. 
In the Midwest, they looooove ketchup on their dogs. 
In Jersey, sauteed peppers, onions, and even potatoes can be found on a Italian Style Hot Dog.
